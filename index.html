A NEW BEGINNING

We're re-building Migblock, from the ground up.

Instead of trying to build everything all at once, we’re trying to build a simple Foundation, that which we may build upon based on your Feedback.

♡ SECTORS ♡

Infinite Space

The In-Game Universe is divided into Sectors. Each Sector contains an entire Solar-System, littered with a multitude of Planets to explore.

♡ WORLDS ♡

Multiple Planets

Travel between Planets within a Sector. Cubic Gravity pulls you to the closest astral body.

♡ MULTIPLAYER ♡

Play Together

Each Sector enhabits it's own logical Simulation, allowing Servers to grow without one busy region becoming a bottleneck.

THE UNIVERSE

In Space "up"

Every astral body exhibits it's own true Cubic Gravity. This Gravity is respected by all Systems, from Block orientation to Entity and Fluid simulations.
